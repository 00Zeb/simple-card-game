His own caveat: not battle-tested yet

The same Laforgia as on the myths slide, three years on: his Substack post “Expectation-Driven Development” (21 March 2026) keeps TDD’s discipline of specifying behaviour before implementation, “without the ceremony”. Our data backs his two demands. Executed evidence, not narrated: one reflection was copied from another model, and one status log cites commit hashes that don’t exist. A different fox: only tests GLM and Gemma didn’t write themselves caught their bugs. His expectations start as plain sentences, and the previous slide says a sentence can be misread; that’s why each one has to be proven by running it. He says himself he hasn’t battle-tested it on a real project.
