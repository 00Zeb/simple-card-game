46% is across the six agents told to do TDD by prompt. GLM returns “Josh wins 1 to 2” when Josh wins 2–1, and its own tests say that is correct: ask the room again what x was. Callback to Laforgia’s quote from the myths slide: by his definition (tests as a tool to design code), design the tests didn’t change isn’t TDD.
