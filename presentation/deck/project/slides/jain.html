“Are Harnesses Worth the Cost?”, martinfowler.com, 13 Oct 2026. A greenfield Java backend-for-frontend, 10 runs per arm. All four setups were judged by the same 178 external acceptance tests: that suite is the referee, not part of the harness. The harness took the cheap model from 3 of 10 to 10 of 10, and harnessed Luna still cost about a third less than unharnessed Sol. His recommendation matches this talk: for frontier models, run the independent check once as a final gate; for cheaper models, run it throughout.
