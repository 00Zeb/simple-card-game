8–13× the cost and 8–22× the time, with no measurable quality gain.

Measured from the session transcripts at list prices. Opus 5 costs $5/$25 per million tokens and Opus 5.5 $4/$20, so TDD by prompt on Opus 5.5 would be about a fifth cheaper. In absolute terms the money is trivial next to an hour of developer time; the point is that nothing measurable came back for it. Two runs of the same model caught 8 of 9 and 5 of 9.
